# Who the site is for

- **Main audience:** first- and second-year students living on the Hill, many in their
  first apartment. They have a windowsill, not a garden. Most have never been to a swap.
- What they worry about: "I don't have anything good to bring", "is it awkward?",
  "do I need to know about plants?"
- They find us from a flyer QR code or a friend's text, **on their phone**, usually
  while walking. They decide in about ten seconds.
- **Not** for: serious collectors or landscaping businesses.
